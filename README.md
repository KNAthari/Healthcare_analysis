# Healthcare Analysis

## Objective
The objective of this healthcare analysis is to find and discover trends within the dataset provided. And to practice analysing data from a healthcare setting.

## Prepare
To prepare for analysis, I, first exported the dataset on Google Sheets in order to familize myself with the dataset and to identify and document irregularities within it. After looking through the dataset I developed six questions to guide my analyses:
    
    1. How many patients were enrolled?
    2. What are the leading medical conditions? At what age range do they occur? And how do they appear by gender?
    3. What is the average billing amount by medical condition? And does it have any correlation with the insurance provider?
    4. Is the average billing amount falling, rising or staying flat year over year?
    5. Has the average length of stay changed?
    6. What is the average age of patients enrolled?

## Process
When looking at the dataset in Google Sheets I decided to format the name column by separating it into two columns `First Name` and `Last Name`. I also formatted the `Billing Amount` column rounding to the nearest two numbers and `Hospital` by removing any trailing comma's and dangling and's within the column. Furthermore, I removed two columns which I found to be useless to my analysis those being the `Test Results` and `Room Number`. Finally, I added a column to the dataset in order to help facilitate my analysis that new column being `Age Range`. I divided the columns as follows:

Teenagers: 13 - 19
Young Adults: 20 - 39
Middle Age: 40 - 64
Senior: 65+

The lowest age in the dataset is 13 years old.
